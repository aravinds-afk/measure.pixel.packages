Lead Generation Plan · BHAP

Getting BHAP More Site Visits and Bookings

A simple, video-led plan across Instagram, Facebook, Google and WhatsApp — designed to bring your projects serious home-buyer enquiries and site visits, not just phone numbers.

The idea, in one line

Nobody books an ₹80 lakh home off a brochure. So we show your projects the way a buyer actually experiences them — the location, the sample flat, the construction progress, the families already living there — to people in your city who are actively looking to buy. Then we make it very easy for them to book a site visit.

Step 1

They see you

Project videos run on Instagram, Facebook and YouTube — shown only to people in your city, in your price band, who are likely to buy in the next few months.

Step 2

They trust you

Price and payment-plan breakdowns, sample-flat tours, construction updates and RERA details answer their doubts before they ever speak to you.

Step 3

They enquire

Three easy ways to reach you — WhatsApp, a one-tap form, or a project page. All three land in one place, with the project and source marked.

Step 4

They visit, you close

Your sales team calls within 5 minutes, books a site visit, and we track which ads actually produced bookings — not just enquiries.

The one thing we ask you to judge us on: not "how many leads", but how many site visits you got, and what each booking cost you. Cheap leads are easy to buy; buyers who walk into your sales office are not. Everything in this plan is built around that difference.

2The videos we will make

This is where the plan lives or dies. A 3D render of a tower looks the same as every other developer's render. A 40-second video of your site head walking through the actual sample flat, tape measure in hand, does not — and that is what makes a buyer visit your project instead of the one down the road.

Price & payment-plan explainers

"What ₹80 lakh actually gets you here." Carpet area, car parking, clubhouse, GST and registration — shown on screen, with the payment schedule.

Our strongest performer

Sample flat tours

Room-by-room walk-through of the show flat or a handed-over unit, with carpet area, BHK and price range on screen. The closest thing to a site visit from a phone.

Proof of quality

Location & connectivity

Drone shot plus a real drive: "6 minutes to the metro, 10 to the IT park, 4 to the school." Location is the first question every buyer asks.

Answers "where is it?"

Buyer guides

"5 mistakes people make before booking a flat" · "Carpet vs super built-up, explained." You or your sales head, on site. Builds authority fast.

Brings new audiences

Construction progress

Monthly drone update and timelapse — slab by slab, floor by floor. Your answer when a buyer asks "will it be delivered on time?"

Protects your credibility

Resident & buyer reviews

Families who bought from you, speaking inside their own homes, in their own language. Name and project on screen.

Closes doubts

Offer videos

"Free site visit with pickup and drop this weekend." Straight to the point, one clear action, with the launch price or limited-unit offer. This is the ad that collects the enquiry.

Does the asking

Follow-up videos

Shown only to people who watched but didn't enquire. Covers bank loan approvals, RERA registration, possession timeline and the booking process.

Second chance

Site diary clips

Raw phone videos from your site and sales office — today's slab, a family's site visit, the weekend crowd. Looks real, costs nothing, and platforms show them cheaply.

Weekly, free
Every video follows the same rules: shot vertical for mobile · the first 3 seconds do the work · subtitles on screen, because most people watch without sound · spoken in the local language, not brochure English · the locality named on screen, because local always beats generic · and the RERA registration number shown on every ad, as the rules require.

What we need from your side

  • One person who answers, fast. Every enquiry called within 5 minutes, weekends included — that is when buyers look. This matters more than anything we do on the ad side.
  • Access to the site and show flat for the two shoot days, plus 3 past buyers willing to speak on camera.
  • 30–40 minutes of your time on camera across the shoot days. Buyers trust the founder's face more than any actor.
  • Honest status updates on each enquiry — called, visited, negotiating, booked or lost. This is how we teach the ads to find more of your actual buyers.
  • Price list, floor plans and RERA details kept current, so every ad and auto-reply is accurate.
  • Patience for 3 months. Long decision cycles mean early months always look worse than the truth.

What you'll get from us

  • A weekly WhatsApp update — enquiries, site visits, cost, what changed.
  • A monthly review call with the full picture, in plain language.
  • Every enquiry in one shared list, with project and source marked, that you own.
