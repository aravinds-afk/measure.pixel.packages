What Success Looks Like

Measured on enquiries, not impressions.

We track the indicators that connect marketing to business outcomes. Month 1 sets the benchmarks; Months 2 and 3 are judged on improvement against them.

01

Relevant enquiries generated

Enquiries that match your service area, project type, and budget range.

02

Cost per enquiry

Advertising spend divided by enquiries, tracked by campaign and creative.

03

Qualified enquiries

Leads your team confirms as serious, reachable, and a fit for SS Constructions.

04

Follow-up completion

The share of enquiries contacted and progressed through the CRM workflow.

05

Site visits booked

Prospects who commit time to see a site, property, or meet your team.

06

Opportunities & projects closed

Tracked where sales outcome data is shared back into the CRM.

We do not promise specific enquiry volumes, cost per lead, site visits, or signed projects. We commit to building the system properly, optimising it every month, and reporting transparently on each of these indicators.
